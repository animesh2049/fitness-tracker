Day before
Soak the rajma in plenty of water overnight, at least 8 hours. The app reminds you at 21:00 the evening before this meal is planned.
Procedure
1
Drain the soaked rajma. Pressure cook with 3 cups water and salt for 5 to 6 whistles until soft.
2
Heat oil, add cumin, then onion. Cook until golden. Add ginger garlic paste and cook 1 minute.
3
Add tomato puree, turmeric, coriander powder, red chilli and garam masala. Cook until oil separates.
4
Add the rajma with its water. Simmer 15 minutes, mash a few beans to thicken. Serve over rice with salad.
